## 2. How Decimation does loot today (read from deobf/src, 13 Oktober 2026)

- `LootTableRegistry` (deci.aD.l) maps 33 blocks to 12 item lists, hardcoded. Lists and their
  blocks (items counted):
  1. general, 94 items (food cans, scrap, melee, packs, casual clothes, 3 pistols): chest,
     wreckage 1..5, metal shelf;
  2. garbage, 5: trash can, dumpster, both skeletons, phone box;
  3. computer parts, 8: monitor;
  4. christmas, 9: presents;
  5. police, 17 (baton, NYPD set, r870, spas12, glock17, m9, suppressors, security keycard):
     police crate, police wrecks;
  6. snacks and drinks, 18: vending machines;
  7. medical, 14 (cure, morphine, blood, first aid kit, lab keycard): medical crate;
  8. explosives, 9 (rockets, grenades): ammo crate;
  9. military, 271 (every gun, every magazine, every military armor set, attachments, military
     keycard, supply drop radio): military crate, military wrecks, care package, mech wreck;
  10. guns and magazines, 174: weapon cabinet;
  11. supply drop, 92 (juggernaut armor, minigun, rpg, glider): supply drop;
  12. ammo boxes, 25: large ammo crate.
- Items per open (FIXED v0.57.0, now 1 .. n and stacks 1 .. n: tools/patches/PatchLootRolls.java,
  bug.md): `IntRange.upTo(n)` picked `1 + nextInt(n - 1)`, so 1 .. n-1, not 1 .. n. A
  military / police / medical / ammo crate and the weapon cabinet (n = 2) always gave exactly 1
  item; care package and large ammo crate (5) 1 to 4; chest (3) 1 to 2; supply drop (8) 1 to 7.
- Rarity is per ITEM, not per pool: the roll weight is the item's own `lootChance` (0.5 for
  Decimation items unless set, 0.9 for vanilla items), the same in every crate. The 271 item
  military list therefore gives a Barrett as often as a magazine.
- Refill: `LootCooldownRegistry` (deci.aB.e) per block position, `lootRespawnTime` 900 s for
  every crate (decimation_server.properties).
- The loot screen: `LootInteractHandler` (deci.aK.k) rolls into an 18 slot inventory, sends it
  (PacketLootInventory) and opens GUI_LOOT.
- NPC drops: Decimation's human NPCs have no drop code of their own. Their gun sits in a field
  of BanditEntity (`Deci.setNpcGun`), so it never drops `[inferred]`; armor is in the vanilla
  equipment slots and may drop at vanilla's 8.5 % `[not verified]`. Walking over items does not
  pick them up (ItemPickupHandler); a right click on the item does.
- Player death leaves `EntityCorpse` (54 slots, 18000 ticks); NPCs do not use it.

## 3. Crate levels (by content type)

### 3.1 Blocks and look
- Four blocks of ours, one per kind (`deciworldgen:crate_military`, `_police`, `_medical`,
  `_civilian`); metadata = level (0..3) x 4 + facing (0..3): 16 crates in 4 block ids.
- Drawn as plain baked blocks (ISimpleBlockRenderingHandler), NOT tile entities: hundreds per
  city (prop lesson 5, docs/prop_placement.md: every tile entity prop is its own model render).
  The loot code keys on the block position, so no tile entity is needed.
- One crate shape per kind, inside one cell: military olive drab with a stencil, police dark blue,
  medical white with a red cross, civilian brown wood / cardboard. A painted band round the crate
  tells the level: L1 grey, L2 green, L3 red, L4 gold `[draft colours]`; L4 also gets a padlock.
  Textures generated by a script of ours (like the gun textures), so they can be committed.

### 3.2 What each level holds (draft lists; the user reviews them)
BUILT v0.57.0 as the built-in lists of `fixes/LootPools` (config/deciworldgen_loot.json, written
on first start; military_1 .. civilian_4). Magazine rows are the magazines each listed gun loads
(read from the gun), so the file holds the exact names. All item names:
docs/references/decimation_items.tsv (dev test `items`).
Weights inside a list: common 10, uncommon 4, rare 1. Items per open and refill `[draft]`:
L1 2 to 3 items, 15 min; L2 1 to 2, 30 min; L3 1, 60 min; L4 4 to 6 (one from each type
first), 120 min.

### 3.3 Where they go (by building value)
- L1: all over the city by building kind (civilian in homes, shops, offices; police in police
  buildings; medical in clinics, pharmacies, hospitals; military in military buildings), where
  BuildingDress already places loot.
- L2: police, medical and military buildings, refuges (armory, clinic), military bases (barracks,
  ASP, motor pool), survivor camps.
- L3: armories and locked rooms: wasteland locked buildings, base arms room, police station
  armory, hospital store room `[draft]`.
- L4: only the rarest places: the bunker armory, supply drops, the best locked wasteland building,
  the base TOC.
- Decimation's own crates stay where they are with their own lists; ours are added beside them,
  except in places where we already choose the loot ourselves (wasteland swaps, bunker armory,
  base arms room), which move to our crates.

### 3.4 How it would be built (loot screen path verified by the spike, 13 Oktober 2026)
- Lists in `config/deciworldgen_loot.json` (docs/systems_plan.md 2.2): kind, level, items with
  weight and count, items per open, refill minutes; the user can edit it.
- Our own interact handler for our crates: rolls with our weights, own refill per level, then
  hands the inventory to Decimation's loot screen with `fixes/Loot.open(player, inventory)`
  (VERIFIED, section 7).
- Guns with attachments: the NBT `<slot>Attach` we already write for NPC rifles (NpcLoadouts).

## 4. NPC loot: the lootable body

### 4.1 How it works
- When a human NPC (bandit, soldier, Soviet / military, all our tiers) dies from damage a player
  dealt, a body stays where it fell. Right click opens the loot screen with its contents (the
  same screen as a crate). Taken items are gone from the body; the body disappears when emptied
  or after 5 minutes.
- NPCs killed only by zombies or other NPCs leave nothing (no farming the enemy spawner from
  behind a wall) `[draft]`.
- Look: the NPC lying down in what it wore (its own renderer turned on its back) or a body bag
  `[inferred: which one is feasible decided later]`. The spike body (fixes/LootBody) is drawn as
  vanilla's default white box.

### 4.2 Contents per tier (draft)
Gun: the gun it carried, with its attachments, magazine partly loaded. Armor: each worn piece by
its own chance. Extras: rolls from a crate pool. Caps: bottlecaps.

## 7. Spike results (13 Oktober 2026, dev test `loot`, all 13 checks PASS)

Both spikes from docs/systems_plan.md section 7 step 0 work; nothing in the design has to change.

1. Our code opens Decimation's loot screen on an inventory of ours: `fixes/Loot.open(player, inv)`
   does what LootInteractHandler does: `Deci.setLootInventory` (PlayerData.setLootInventory
   deci.Q.b.a, then PacketLootInventory deci.aE.a$R to the client), and 2 ticks later
   `Deci.openLootGui` (openGui GUI_LOOT deci.Q.c.Xa with x -1, y 2; y is the container type, 2 =
   with loot slots, PlayerGearContainer deci.f.d). Our own tick queue does the delay (Decimation's
   TickScheduler is drained by a ServerProxy handler).
2. The loot slots of the server container are Slots on that SAME InventoryBasic object, so a stack
   taken leaves our inventory directly: no copy back needed. Checked through the client's real
   window clicks (pick up, put into the main inventory).
3. A body entity (`fixes/LootBody`, registered as deciworldgen:loot_body, entity id 0) opens it on
   the client's real interact packet (interactFirst); reopened, it shows only what is left; it goes
   2 ticks after the last stack is taken (checked every 20 ticks); items survive NBT save / load.
4. Limits: the setLootInventory parameter is an InventoryBasic (not any IInventory), 18 slots fill
   the loot area (4 rows by 5, 18 used); our path does not skip creative players (Decimation's crate
   handler does). Test trap: the dev test harness closes every screen each tick unless
   `DevAutoTest.keepScreen` is set; the closed client screen does not close the server container.
5. Duplication guard (found by a commit security review, same day): Decimation's loot container
   never checks its source (canInteractWith always true), so a screen left open on a body that is
   gone, or saved away with its chunk, would hand out items a second time. `Loot.closeFor` closes
   every loot screen on a body's inventory when the body dies (setDead) and, each tick, for a player
   farther than LootBody.RANGE (8). Checked: a screen open on a body closes on both sides 1 tick after
   the body goes; the range close uses the same call `[not verified separately]`. Rule for every
   loot source of ours (crates too): close its screens when the source goes.
Evidence: docs/shots/loot_spike_v0.56/sheet_loot.png (shots_index.md).
